# Step 10: complete unsent email preview

`scripts/etf_mail_preview.py` accepts a saved production metadata/collection manifest/pre-build history triple and a separate research bundle. It reuses the production email HTML renderer, checks the saved subject/date, and calls the existing delivery validator with candidate auditing enabled before composing the preview. Corrupt saved body hashes, market-session metadata, missing links or undisclosed source warnings refuse the preview. This does not send mail or alter production generation.

Each research bundle includes materials, exact cards, model reviews, per-claim editor records, optional exact appeals, date/scope notes bound to material IDs, and reasons for pending sources. Editorial packets are reconstructed using the existing validator rather than accepting a caller-supplied PASS. Failed or pending cards do not display their claim text. Duplicate sources, stale cards/reviews/editors, missing source notes and unknown material IDs fail closed. Source links are checked and all inserted text is HTML escaped.

The added research appendix displays all reviewed Chinese facts, source limitations and follow-up questions, with clickable original links and explicit saved publication dates. It does not print English source quotations or full article bodies. The pending list has reasons and reading links. The complete original saved email follows unchanged: its market tables, article selection, warnings and audit text are all retained, rendered from the original body whose hash must match the saved manifest. Original HTML presentation is regenerated using the same production renderer; the original Markdown is unchanged. The original article placeholders are **not** silently replaced, and the preview explicitly identifies them as the original saved version. Inline article replacements and history updates are both zero.

The candidate date/history diagnostic uses the frozen collection cutoff (not today's wall clock), timezone-aware original publication fields, the current production 36-hour primary/14-day research-backfill/365-day duplicate constants, and captured candidate identity. URL or title matches in pre-build history remain duplicates. Fixed blogs/podcasts do not receive the research-backfill exemption. Future/naive dates, unmatched identities and unresolved date notes are held. These diagnostic rules do not assert topic/product eligibility or grant delivery permission. Editorial completion and all-date checks still produce only research samples, never permission to send.

Actual sample: the 2026-10-20 saved report, with market data through 2026-10-19, plus five completed research cards and three pending materials from step7. Four completed materials are historical; the arXiv capture's 2026-10-19 announcement field and the v3 page's 2026-10-15 revision date have different roles, so its current-news qualification remains unresolved. Bridgewater's saved date and page date discrepancy is retained. One historical completed podcast also matches prior sent history. All five completed cards are held from new-current-news use and appear only in the labeled research appendix. The saved original still discloses its FAILED content audit (7 failures,119 gaps); generating this preview does not resolve that audit.

Outputs are `mail-preview.html` and `mail-preview-audit.json`, in a required new directory. Audit fields include snapshot/run/body/input hashes, recomputed editorial packet IDs, date/history diagnostics, source warning counts and fixed `delivery_eligible:false`, `sent:false`. The CLI deliberately emits no `metadata.json` suitable for a mail sender. It imports no sender, requests no model output, and never modifies source artifacts or sent history. No production file, schedule or credential changes are involved.

Validation: 35 related local tests and 8 subtests passed, including full original rendering preservation, candidate-date separation, history duplicate checks, future/naive dates, corrupt saved body/market/history, withheld rejected claims, stale editor records, pending materials, escaped HTML/unsafe source links and refusal to overwrite an existing output folder. The real preview rendered successfully as an 89,194-byte HTML file with Windows newlines. Static checks confirmed balanced HTML, all 77 links, five research cards, identical offline replay and complete retention of the original rendered body. Browser visual inspection was blocked by the browser's URL policy rejecting local `file:` URLs; no visual or real email-client compatibility check is claimed.

Example:

```powershell
python scripts/etf_mail_preview.py --bundle preview-input.json --metadata saved/metadata.json --manifest saved/collection_manifest.json --history-before saved/history_before.json --out-dir new-preview
```

The source/date notes are explicit editorial annotations, not authenticated external evidence. The bundle and hash ledger are not a signing service. Remaining work includes resolving current-news dates/content, generating reviewed current-eligible cards and evaluating an opt-in production adapter without weakening existing gates.
